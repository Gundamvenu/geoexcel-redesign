Sustainability

Our approach and the principles we follow

The company’s focus on the UN 17 Sustainable Development Goals and CSR dates from 2019. This is what Geo Excel publishes about its approach.

Our approach

  • Uplifting livelihood and ensuring good working conditions
  • Implementing living wages for all stakeholders
  • Climate change
  • Building an environmentally sustainable business

Principles we follow

  • We follow sustainable agricultural methods of collection of natural raw materials.
  • We source natural raw material using proper harvesting methods, sourced at the right time for the right price.
  • We support the preservation of endangered plant species and avoid use of them.
  • We practise the organic farming process and avoid the use of pesticides and chemicals.
  • We avoid raw materials that may affect climate conditions.

Statements are the company’s own, from its public website. Audits or certifications supporting these practices are not published on the current site.
