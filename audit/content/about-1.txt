Principles we follow

​

We follow sustainable agricultural methods of collection of natural raw materials.

We source natural raw material using proper harvesting methods, Sourced at the right time for the right price.

We support in the preservation of endangered plant species and avoid use of them. 

We practise the organic farming process and avoid the use of pesticides and chemicals.

We avoid raw materials that may effect the climate conditions.
