ABOUT US

GEOEXCEL

With a rich heritage stemming since the 1980s, the name ‘GeoExcel’, short for Geographic Excellence,

was officially brought to life in 2023.

From growing rice, sugarcane, sesame, to a single cement and steel dealership, today, the GeoExcel Group has multiple verticals spanning agriculture, automotive, construction and trading.

1980's

Agriculture
Started our journey as farmers engaged in growing and harvesting of rice, sugarcane and sesame.

1990's

Cement & Steel Dealers
Diversification into the cement & steel business. This provided us with a strong foundation to commence civil contracting services.

1993

-

2014

Civil Construction Activities
In 1993 we forayed into turn-key civil construction services for the residential sector in Bangalore, India.
By 2014 we expanded into commercial, industrial and infrastructure civil construction with a small mix of our own developments under the name "Anuadi Constructions"

2019

Sustainable Development Goals & CSR focus on achieving the "United Nations 17 Sustainability Goals" and a passion driven approach to CSR activities.

2023

Birth of "GEO EXCEL"
We engage in the export trade of the products listed on our website.
