About

Geographic Excellence, with a farming heritage

With a heritage stemming from the 1980s, the name “GeoExcel”, short for Geographic Excellence, was officially brought to life in 2023. From growing rice, sugarcane and sesame to a single cement and steel dealership, the GeoExcel Group today has multiple verticals spanning agriculture, automotive, construction and trading.

  1. 1980s

    Agriculture

    Started as farmers growing and harvesting rice, sugarcane and sesame.

  2. 1990s

    Cement & steel dealers

    Diversified into cement and steel, a foundation for civil contracting services.

  3. 1993–2014

    Civil construction

    Turn-key residential construction in Bangalore from 1993; by 2014 commercial, industrial and infrastructure work, plus own developments under the name “Anuadi Constructions”.

  4. 2019

    SDGs & CSR

    Focus on the UN 17 Sustainable Development Goals and a passion-driven approach to CSR.

  5. 2023

    Birth of “Geo Excel”

    Export trade of the products listed on this website.
