How it works

From specification to quote request in three steps

  1. Shortlist

    Open any product to see its specification sheet: pack sizes, region, moisture, HS code.

  2. Add to enquiry

    Press “Add to enquiry” on each product. Your list follows you across the site.

  3. Send one request

    Tell us your company, destination and volumes. Your selected products are filled in for you.

What helps us quote accurately

  • Product and variety, grade or size
  • Pack size and packaging type
  • Approximate quantity per shipment
  • Destination country or port
  • Any documents you need from the exporter
  • Your company name and a reachable email

Heritage

Four decades from the farm to export trade

“Geo Excel” is short for Geographic Excellence, officially brought to life in 2023.

  1. 1980s

    Agriculture

    Started as farmers growing and harvesting rice, sugarcane and sesame.

  2. 1990s

    Cement & steel dealers

    Diversified into cement and steel, a foundation for civil contracting services.

  3. 1993–2014

    Civil construction

    Turn-key residential construction in Bangalore from 1993; by 2014 commercial, industrial and infrastructure work, plus own developments under the name “Anuadi Constructions”.

  4. 2019

    SDGs & CSR

    Focus on the UN 17 Sustainable Development Goals and a passion-driven approach to CSR.

  5. 2023

    Birth of “Geo Excel”

    Export trade of the products listed on this website.
